# Contracts and payment methods

Read this before changing household contracts or payment methods. `AGENTS.md` only points here. There are no MCP or CLI contract commands in v1.

## Contracts

`app/services/contracts.py`. Table `contracts` (name, required provider, optional `plan_name`, `service`, `bill_delivery`, optional delivery detail and notes, optional `started_on`, optional `ended_on`). Active means `ended_on` is null. There is no `is_active` flag and no reopen in v1: a save cannot clear `ended_on`. `service` is `electricity`, `health_insurance`, `internet`, `phone`, `car_insurance`, or `other` (a junk drawer — not a budget category). `bill_delivery` is `email`, `post`, `app`, or `other`. Text fields are stripped, length-capped, and reject a run of 6+ digits. Dates use `date.fromisoformat`. `ended_on` cannot be in the future (`Australia/Brisbane` today) and must be on or after `started_on` when both are set. End sets `ended_on` to that Brisbane today and does not deactivate linked bills or change `next_due_date`; the confirm copy says bills keep being paid and forecast. Hard-delete is 409 while any recurring bill, active or inactive, still has `contract_id` set. `recurring_bills.contract_id` and `transactions.contract_id` are nullable, indexed, `ON DELETE SET NULL`. SQLite foreign keys are off, so delete also nulls payment stamps in the app; tests assert the 409, not SET NULL. Bill HTML and JSON change `contract_id` only when the field is present: omit leaves it, null clears it, a new id must be an active contract, and re-saving the bill's current ended contract is allowed. The stamp is copied once, at confirm (`budget_expense` when paid from a budget, `regular` when paid from a fund), pay (`regular`), and scheduler auto-pay (`regular`). It is not rewritten when the bill's contract later changes, and it is not backfilled. The clawback row (`type` `expense`, `transaction_type` `withdrawal`, `recurring_bill_id` set) stays null. Forecast, cover, monthly cost, and which bills the scheduler pays do not read contracts. A plan change is end + new contract + relink the same bill. No MCP or CLI contract commands. Spend totals are schema-only in v1.

## Payment methods

`app/services/payment_methods.py`. Table `payment_methods` (nickname, kind `card`/`bank`/`manual`/`wallet`, last 4, card expiry, owner, archived, `wallet_provider`, `funding_method_id`). `RecurringBill.payment_method_id` is optional. A card is valid through the last day of its expiry month. The Expires control stays `<input type="month">`. `parse_month_input` accepts `YYYY-MM`, `MM/YY`, `MM/YYYY`, `MM-YY`, and `MM-YYYY` (a single-digit month is fine). Two-digit years are 2000–2099. Years outside 2000–2100 and months outside 1–12 are rejected, and the HTML form shows that message in `gw-msg-error`. `PAYMENT_METHOD_EXPIRY_WARN_DAYS` (default 60, clamp 1–365) is read on each call. The dashboard banner (`id="payment-methods-banner"`, under Bills cover, any month) has one line per in-use method that is an expired or expiring card, the old side of an open replacement, or archived while a bill still uses it. Replacing a method is a saved `payment_method_replacements` job (`open`/`finished`/`stopped`) with one item per in-use bill. Ticking moves that bill immediately. Finish asks, then archives the old method only when no active bill is still on it. Stop leaves bills where they are. Nothing finishes on its own. Bills due within 7 days that are still on the old method are flagged. Web, JSON, and MCP share the service. MCP cannot delete a method or write a replacement. Nicknames, owners, and notes reject a run of 6+ digits after spaces, hyphens, and dots between digits are removed. Errors do not repeat the value.

A wallet is Apple Pay (`apple_pay`) or Google Pay (`google_pay`). It is its own method, linked to exactly one card through `funding_method_id` (indexed, no `ON DELETE`). `wallet_provider` and `funding_method_id` are required together for a wallet and null for every other kind, and the funding id cannot be the wallet's own id (check constraint). The card must be a card, and on create or when the link changes it must not be archived. Saving the same funding id again is allowed after that card is archived. A wallet stores no last 4 and no expiry; sending either is rejected. `effective_expiry` is the one helper behind the banner, the Expires column, the list badge, and the bill badge: a wallet uses its card's month and year. Banner sentences stay as they are (owner, "end of Month Year", bill names, expired and expiring apart). A wallet whose card is archived uses that same archived sentence. If bills also use the card, the card still has its own line. Labels read "Apple Pay (Everyday Visa ending 4321)". The card's edit page says "Also used by: Apple Pay". The bill picker lists wallets with that label. The form shows Provider and Linked card for a wallet and hides Expires. JSON accepts and returns `wallet_provider` and `funding_method_id`, plus `effective_expiry_month` and `effective_expiry_year`. A nickname edit keeps the link. Changing a card's kind is refused while any wallet, archived or not, points at it. Changing a wallet to another kind clears the provider and the link. Archiving a card that only funds wallets is allowed, with no extra confirm. Hard-delete is 409 with "A wallet still links to this." while any wallet points at the method, and that reason hides Delete. SQLite foreign keys are off, so the app enforces it.

Replacing a card that funds non-archived wallets can start with no active bills, and the new method must be a card. Each of those wallets gets its own checklist row ("Add <new card> to Apple Pay on your phone, then tick"). Sync picks up a wallet added while the job is open, the same way it picks up a bill. Ticking repoints that wallet's `funding_method_id` immediately. Nothing relinks a wallet on its own. `payment_method_replacement_items.recurring_bill_id` is nullable; `wallet_method_id` is the other target; exactly one is set, and each is unique per job. The replacement JSON item has `kind` `bill` or `wallet` and the wallet fields. Finish is 409 while any active bill or non-archived wallet is still on the old card, then archives. A job that is only wallets can finish. Stop leaves ticked wallets on the new card, and the stop copy says so. Replacing a wallet itself only moves its bills, and the new-method form defaults to a card. MCP reads include the wallet fields and the effective expiry. MCP writes refuse `kind` `wallet` with "Add a wallet on the payment methods page." and leave the provider and link in place on any other update.
