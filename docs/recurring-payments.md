# Recurring payments

Read this before changing confirm, skip, pay, or `paid_from`. `AGENTS.md` only points here. Contract stamping is summarized here and specified in `docs/contracts-and-payment-methods.md`. Overflow debit rules depend on month-end settlement in `docs/monthly-processing.md`.

## Recurring payments

`app/services/recurring_payments.py`. Each bill has `paid_from` (`bills_fund` by default, `budget`, or `sinking_fund` with `paid_from_fund_id`). Only Bills-fund rows feed the forecast, the recommended amount, the cover banner, and the scheduler. Weekly and fortnightly step by 7 and 14 days. `iter_bill_due_dates` stops after `end_date`; confirm, skip, and the scheduler do too. Payments from a budget or another fund, and variable Bills-fund bills, wait in Due to confirm. Confirm is dated on the due date. A budget confirm raises `spent_amount`. It debits overflow only when that due month's `household_months.settlement_outcome` is `surplus`, `shortfall`, or `zero` and the stored fund still exists and is not soft-deleted. The debit is the confirm amount, from that stored fund, not from today's overflow setting. Old months with no sweep record, `no_overflow`, and `unresolved` record the payment and do not debit. Skip writes no transaction. `GET /api/bills/due`, `POST /api/bills/{id}/confirm`, and `POST /api/bills/{id}/skip` share the service with the web UI, MCP, and CLI. Confirm, pay, and scheduler auto-pay copy `bill.contract_id` onto the payment (see Contracts). The overflow clawback withdrawal does not.

## Occurrence transitions

`transition_occurrence` is the only settler for confirm, skip, manual pay, and auto-pay. It flushes and does not commit. HTML, JSON, and MCP commit once after it returns. Auto-pay is the only caller that commits per bill, after its savepoint releases. One bill's failure rolls back that savepoint only. Confirm calls `ensure_backfill` before the transition; that commit does not contain the cursor move, the payment, or the clawback. Skip, manual pay, and auto-pay do not call it.

The occurrence is `next_due_date` when the compare-and-set runs. A payment stores it as `occurrence_due_date`. Pay, Confirm, and auto-pay are the only writers of that column. Skip writes no row. A hand-entered transaction does not settle an occurrence, whether or not it names a bill. A one-off Bills fund expense with no bill id is a normal transaction: it debits the fund, stays fully editable and deletable, and does not move any cursor or stop auto-pay. Recording the bank debit that way, and leaving the cursor, means the startup catch-up will also debit the Bills fund.

Deleting either row of a linked pair (the payment or the clawback that points at it with `adjustment_for_transaction_id`) reverses both and deletes both. `next_due_date` and `last_auto_settled_on` stay put. A partner that is already gone is a one-row reverse. Rows with both new columns null, including old take-backs, stay a one-row delete. They are not paired by the description.

An HTML save of a bill is one conditional update of the edited columns, `WHERE next_due_date` is the date the form loaded (`next_due_date_was`). A missing or invalid date saves nothing. JSON `PUT` and MCP `update_bill` require `expected_next_due_date` when `next_due_date` is sent. Omitting the date leaves the cursor alone and still saves the other fields. `last_auto_settled_on` is not client-writable.

`last_auto_settled_on` is the auto-pay daily slot only. Manual pay does not read or write it. A fixed bill that is several periods overdue can be debited twice on a day the person also presses Pay: once for the date they paid, once for the next date auto-pay takes. That second debit is money that is still due.

The late-confirm clawback is unchanged. It debits the stored settlement fund by the confirm amount when the outcome is `surplus`, `shortfall`, or `zero` and that fund still exists. The description stays `Taken back for {name} ({Month Year} {category})`. The overlap described in `household_months.py` is not closed: a confirm that overlaps settlement can still miss the debit, and a concurrent bill can still save a stale fund balance over settlement. The Bills fund is not the overflow fund.
