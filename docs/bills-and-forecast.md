# Bills allocation, forecast, cover, and fund runway

Read this before changing bills allocation, the bills forecast, bills cover, or fund runway. `AGENTS.md` only points here.

## Bills allocation

Recommended = (Total Annual Bill Cost / 12).

Annual multipliers: weekly 52.143, fortnightly 26.071, every 28 days 13.036, monthly 12, quarterly 4, yearly 1, every 2 years 0.5. Every 2 years is half the amount per year, so the monthly figure on the bills page and the recommended deposit is the amount divided by 24. `/monthly-cost` stays recorded spending and does not use this multiplier. The 12-month forecast and bills cover (3 to 12 months) include the full amount in the month the bill is due, and leave the other months alone. An unknown frequency raises `UnknownFrequencyError` (see `docs/recurring-payments.md`). The forecast page shows that error. Cover does not skip the bill as a bad date.

## Bills forecast

`generate_bills_forecast` (page `/sinking-funds/{id}/forecast` only; no JSON or MCP consumer) projects active bills with `iter_bill_due_dates`. `contribution` is money still to come and the only primary contribution added into `closing_balance`, which starts from the fund's `current_balance`. If this month already has any `income_allocation` on the Bills fund, the first row keeps `contribution` at 0, sets `contribution_received` True, and sets `received_contribution` to the sum of those deposits. Later rows keep the flag False. The page prints that sum with a "received" note. The Monthly Contribution summary uses `configured_contribution` (fixed amount, or recommended annual cost / 12) rather than the first row's still-to-come figure. Bills cover does not read these rows. `include_secondary` (page query `?secondary=1` only; default off; not stored) adds each row's `secondary` = max(0, Bills secondary goal − `secondary_income_allocation` recorded on that fund in that row's month), including future-dated rows, into `closing_balance`. `secondary_received` is that recorded sum for display and is not added again. `income_allocation` rows are not secondary progress. The goal is the live sum from `app/services/bills_secondary.py` for the same non-deleted fund named Bills. If that fund is missing or the goal is 0, the toggle is hidden and nothing is added. A mid-month goal edit does not claw back `current_balance`. The HTMX swap replaces the summary bar (including 12-Month Low) and the table. Secondary is its own column (`+$X (secondary)`, plus `$Y recorded` when that month has a recording) and is omitted while the toggle is off. The primary-deposit check is unchanged. Bills cover still subtracts recorded secondary for the current month only.

## Bills cover (dashboard)

`app/services/bills_cover.py` looks ahead `BILLS_COVER_HORIZON_MONTHS` calendar months (default 3, clamp 1–12; end date is today plus that many months). Obligations are active recurring-bill occurrences from `next_due_date` through the horizon — including an overdue `next_due_date` — walked with `iter_bill_due_dates` / `advance_due_date` (same frequency rules as `generate_bills_forecast`), plus unpaid `expense` transactions with `recurring_bill_id` set, `date <= horizon end` (past dates still count), and either no sinking fund or the Bills fund. The same bill and due date is counted once, using the unpaid ledger amount. Scheduler bill rows are `is_paid=True` by default and already reduce the Bills balance, so they are not unpaid obligations. Without-secondary cash is the Bills balance plus remaining primary Bills contributions: this month's amount if no `income_allocation` to Bills has posted yet, plus each later 1st on or before the horizon end. With-secondary cash adds each intersecting month's Bills secondary `goal_amount`, minus `secondary_income_allocation` already recorded to Bills in the current month only (future months use the full goal). The dashboard banner is shown only when the without-secondary path is short, and then prints both figures. The sinking-funds list no longer has the 30-day buffer warning. Bills cover does not replace `/sinking-funds/{id}/forecast`.

## Fund runway

`app/services/fund_runway.py`, shown only on the sinking-fund detail page (above the month navigation, as of today, ignoring `?month=&year=`). Net rate is `Transaction.type` only (`income`/`transfer` in, anything else out) for that fund's rows over the last 6 complete calendar months; the current month is excluded and a month with no rows counts as zero. History starts at the earlier of `created_at` (naive values treated as UTC, then `Australia/Brisbane`) and the earliest transaction date; the first counted month is the first full month on or after that date (a fund created on the 1st counts that month). Fewer than 2 months used shows no number. Balance > 0 and net burn > 0 shows whole months rounded down, a run-out month, a warning under 6 months, "less than a month" under 1, and "more than 10 years" over 120. Balance 0 and still burning is an empty warning. Balance < 0 is a debt: net inflow shows a payoff rounded up on a neutral card (same 120-month cap); otherwise a warning that it isn't being paid down. The Bills fund shows a Forecast pointer and no figure. Soft-deleted funds render nothing. Uses the stored `current_balance` (MCP balance drift is unchanged). No new tables, migrations, or env vars.
